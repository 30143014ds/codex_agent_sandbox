# Review guide

## Trends

Use complete attempts, latest revision per attempt and the same instrument identity. Report dates, windows and observations before an interpretation. A score change can reflect context, reporting, overlapping time windows or ordinary fluctuation. No fixed score change is configured as a validated reliable-change threshold. Do not average the three questionnaires together.

If safety items worsen, discuss them directly even when the total improves. If a low total conflicts with severe functional problems, take the problems seriously. Lack of measurement is unknown, not stability or improvement.

## Barriers

Check fit, instructions, task size, fatigue/health, time, resources, fear, competing responsibilities and the user's preferences. Ask whether the exercise addressed the actual concern. Simplify, choose another method, make space for support, or encourage qualified care when appropriate. Record the adjustment and what would make it worth continuing.

## Maintenance plan

Identify early personal signs, typical triggers, methods that have helped, basic supports, a first small action, who can help and when to seek professional care. Plan for predictable difficult days without treating them as proof of failure. Consider a small coping card with a credible reminder and a next action.

Rehearse an ordinary foreseeable setback at a tolerable intensity if wanted. Avoid frightening predictions of relapse or intense evocative work. A maintenance plan should increase the user's independent choices, not dependency on the agent's availability.

## Feedback

Ask a brief open question about usefulness/accuracy or pace when appropriate. If the user says the agent misunderstood or pushed too hard, acknowledge the specific mismatch, correct the formulation and adapt. Do not defend the protocol at the expense of collaboration.
