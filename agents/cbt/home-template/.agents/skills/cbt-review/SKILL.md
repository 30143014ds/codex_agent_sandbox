# Progress and maintenance review

Use `$cbt-notes` to retrieve the relevant period and run the workspace's `due`/`trend` commands. Read [review-guide.md](references/review-guide.md) for trend limits, barriers and maintenance planning. Review only what is available; absence of a record is not evidence that the user did nothing.

Ask what feels different in daily life and what matters now. Compare complete measurements of the same instrument/version alongside functioning, sleep/energy, relationships, meaningful actions and the user's account. Keep episode ratings separate from questionnaire totals. Do not invent statistical significance, a remission diagnosis or an attribution of change to the agent.

Explore exercises as experiments: what happened, what was learned, and what got in the way? If a plan was too demanding, poorly chosen or unwelcome, revise it. Persistent difficulty is not proof of resistance. Check the quality of your understanding and the working relationship before prescribing more homework.

For worsening, ask about relevant changes, functioning and safety without waiting for the next scheduled questionnaire. Support professional help as appropriate. Do not recommend medication adjustments.

Agree on what to continue, change or stop, and one next review point. Save a `review` record and update the care plan/handover. Do not call an ordinary check-in a completed four-week review unless the review was actually carried out.

Sources: Burns chapters 2, 10–16; CCI self-management; selected later TEAM feedback/recurrence planning. Outcome claims for human CBT do not validate this agent.
