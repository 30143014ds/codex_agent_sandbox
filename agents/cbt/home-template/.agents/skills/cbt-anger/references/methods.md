# Anger methods

1. **Costs and benefits:** what does the anger protect or communicate, and what do the current actions cost? The target may be a chosen lower intensity or different action, not eliminating anger.
2. **Hot-to-cool thoughts:** write the charged interpretation and a credible alternative. Preserve a genuine breach: “They did miss the agreement; I can address that without deciding they never care.”
3. **Fairness/rule check:** distinguish a preference, an agreement, a legal/ethical obligation and a prediction. A valid rule does not guarantee compliance; plan for what can actually happen.
4. **Expectation adjustment:** anticipate a recurring difficulty so surprise adds less fuel, while deciding what boundary or action remains necessary. Acceptance of reality is not approval.
5. **Images:** notice repetitive revenge or humiliation imagery; try a brief coping rehearsal at a tolerable intensity. Do not intensify violent imagery or conduct trauma exposure.
6. **Accurate empathy:** consider plausible motives/perspectives as hypotheses, not excuses or claims to know another mind. Skip if it distracts from immediate protection.
7. **Negotiation:** specify the behavior, impact, request and available next step. Reinforce actual helpful cooperation openly and respectfully.
8. **Rehearsal:** practice a low-intensity version of a trigger, a pause and the chosen response. Increase difficulty only if the user wants and tolerates it.

Track triggers, duration, peak intensity and behavior separately. No reduction in anger is required for an assertive action to count as useful. Use Novaco periodically for a broader within-person comparison, not as an episode rating.
